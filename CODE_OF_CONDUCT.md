# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when mistakes happen, and learn from feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through the repository's contact or private reporting channels. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue. Reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
